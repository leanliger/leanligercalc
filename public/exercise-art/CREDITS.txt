Changes: the original SVG drawings were rasterized to WebP and resized
(128 px thumbnails, 384 px drawings), and two of the three drawings per
exercise were selected. Files are renamed to the app's exercise ids.

machine-triceps-extension*.webp, single-arm-incline-db-curl*.webp and
single-arm-preacher-curl*.webp come straight from Everkinetic
(github.com/everkinetic/data, exercises 0210 "Triceps extensions using
machine", 0214 "Alternating incline curl with dumbbell" and 0237 "One arm
preacher curl with dumbbell", same license), rasterized and resized the
same way.

incline-machine-press*.webp are not from Workout Guide or Everkinetic:
they were made from a drawing supplied by the app's owner, and are not
covered by this notice.

The Workout Guide and Everkinetic drawings, as changed here, are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0
license. The rest of this project is not covered by this notice.
